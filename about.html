Origins & Generational Heritage • Est. 2007, Udaipur

Rooted in Shrinath Marketing Company & M M Plylam

Established in 2007 in Udaipur, Rajasthan under the respected trading identities of Shrinath Marketing Company (SMC) and M M Plylam, our journey began with an uncompromising conviction: architects, interior designers, and homeowners deserve building materials of certified purity and lasting integrity.

Over the course of 17+ years, we have earned the enduring trust of generations of clients across Udaipur and Southern Rajasthan. From palatial private estates and boutique heritage resorts to contemporary residential towers, we have stood as the premier regional distribution house for India’s finest plywood and decorative surfaces.

Presenting our specialized proprietary lines ROAR Commercial and ROAR Decor, alongside recognized distribution of Greenply, Keto Ply, and curated calibrated regional timber mills, our flagship ROAR DOORS brand represents the culmination of this legacy — precision calibrated timber engineered for timeless architectural spaces.

Guiding Tenets

Our Core Values

The timeless principles that dictate every log we select, every veneer we peel, and every door leaf we calibrate.

Uncompromising Calibration

We reject the industry tolerance of ±0.5mm. Every ROAR panel is measured on 4 axes to maintain an extraordinary ±0.1mm plane, ensuring immaculate veneer adhesion without rippling.

Generational Forestry

We source only legally harvested, FSC-certified mature timber from managed estates. For every tree harvested, five hardwood saplings are planted in our affiliated agro-forestry reserves.

Molecular Bonding

Our boiling-water-proof panels utilize pure unextended Bordeaux phenolic resins. This creates irreversible cross-linking that laughs at coastal monsoon humidity and boiling water tests.

Architectural Partnership

We do not treat architects as customers; we treat them as co-creators. We provide custom CAD/BIM shop drawings, bespoke height capabilities, and batch grain matching for singular projects.

Precision Engineering

Our 5-Stage Manufacturing Journey

How raw plantation timber transforms into architectural-grade panels and doors of peerless integrity.

01

Log Sourcing & Conditioning

Aged hardwood logs undergo hydrostatic steaming at 70°C to soften lignin fibers before micro-peeling, ensuring velvety, continuous veneer sheets.

02

Core Alignment & Vacuum Treatment

Veneers are staggered and vacuum-impregnated with non-toxic organic borates to render them permanently inedible to borers and termites.

03

Hydraulic Hot-Press Molecular Fusion

Unextended phenolic resin is cured under 14 kg/cm² pressure at 155°C, fusing veneers at a molecular level with zero core voids.

04

Quad-Head Calibration & Sanding

Wide-belt computerized Italian sanders calibrate both surfaces within ±0.1mm tolerance, achieving a silk-smooth finish ready for finishes.

05

Destructive Hydrostatic Lab Testing

Random sample sheets from every batch undergo mandatory 72-hour continuous boiling water immersion and knife-shear bond strength verification.
