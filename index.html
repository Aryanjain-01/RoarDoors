Generational Philosophy • Est. 2007, Udaipur

Rooted in Shrinath Marketing Company & M M Plylam

Established in 2007 in the city of Udaipur under the venerable banners of Shrinath Marketing Company (SMC) and M M Plylam, our enterprise was founded upon an uncompromising principle: providing verified structural timber and world-class decorative surfaces that endure for generations.

Over 17+ years, we have served as the trusted timber partner to hundreds of distinguished architects, interior designers, commercial developers, and prominent homeowners across Udaipur and Southern Rajasthan. Today, through our proprietary ROAR Commercial wall panel division and ROAR Decor decorative surface collection, alongside trusted stockists of Greenply and Keto Ply, our flagship ROAR DOORS atelier delivers an extraordinary standard of architectural joinery.

"We supply timber panels and bespoke doors not merely to satisfy building codes, but to outlive the estates that house them."

The Four Pillars

Why ROAR DOORS Endures

Engineered through uncompromising chemical mastery, automated calibration, and generational timber expertise.

Premium Calibrated Quality

Every sheet undergoes 4-side micro-sanding and automated thickness calibration within ±0.1mm, ensuring zero surface telegraphing under thin veneers.

Termite & Borer Shield

Deep vacuum-pressure impregnation (VPI) infuses organic borate preservatives into every cellular pore, ensuring lifetime immunity against wood pests.

72h Boiling Hydro-Shield

Bonded with 100% unextended Bordeaux phenolic resin cured at 155°C, easily withstanding 72+ continuous hours in boiling water without delamination.

Up to 25-Year Warranty

Our confidence is sealed with a legal, documented replacement guarantee against delamination, warping, and insect damage for up to 25 years.
